ui-sim — ticker の画面シミュレータ (docs/ui-sim.md)

  ui-sim [render] [--scenario FILE] [--out DIR] [--layout glass|dock|classic]
                  [--bg FILE.BMP [--bg NEXT.BMP]] [--name NAME] [--no-gif]
      NAME.png (1x), NAME@3x.png (3x nearest), NAME.gif (scroll + slide transition)
  ui-sim sheet [--scenario FILE] [--out DIR] --bg A.BMP --bg B.BMP ... [--name NAME]
      NAME.png: layouts (glass / dock / classic) x backgrounds, 2x. --bg gradient = built-in gradient
  ui-sim samples [--out samples/]
      regenerate the procedural sample BMPs
